## How to read the evidence

[Structured review](stadium-naming-review.json) contains 106 bounded own notes (the original 90 preserved), exact primary URLs, locators, observation date, source/subject dates where established, and explicit season applicability. It reuses previously reviewed [primary ground notes](primary-ground-evidence.json) and [naming events](supplemental-naming-events.json), without rewriting those source-faithful records. The 34 ground reviews and 60 membership contexts have complete identity coverage; a context gap is not a claim that the stadium name is unknowable.

A dated report supports a printed name for that occasion. A seasonal UEFA directory supports the directory label for that edition, not every match venue or a legal naming interval. A current contact page or historical home-ground continuity cannot automatically prove an unchanged commercial name in a selected season. A planned schedule establishes planned name usage only. Names marked official unspecified are not silently classified as traditional or commercial; competition-clean names remain unverified unless explicitly established. No expiry or exact rename day is inferred from an announcement or contract duration.
