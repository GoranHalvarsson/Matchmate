## New primary evidence and interpretation

- [Former naming sponsor KCOM](https://www.kcom.com/news/happy-20th-birthday-to-the-mkm-stadium/) explicitly identifies Kingston Communications Stadium at the 18 December 2002 opening. That proves opening-name usage, not the agreement’s signing or effective day.
- [Coventry operator anniversary](https://www.coventrybuildingsocietyarena.co.uk/news/coventry-building-society-arena-celebrates-20-years) explicitly identifies Ricoh Arena at the first public event on 20 August 2005. Its [CBS announcement](https://www.coventrybuildingsocietyarena.co.uk/news/wasps-group-agree-major-new-stadium-naming-rights-deal) describes the first name change since opening. Together with the existing 2021 CBS evidence, this supports a year-level commercial continuity assertion from 2005 through the 2021 announcement context. Publication year is not supplied on the page, and this is not a silently extended assertion through the future July effective day. Actual end remains unknown in the conservative period record.
- The [Government Olympic Executive map](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a79017eed915d04220670da/City_of_Coventry_Stadium.pdf), dated 26 October 2010, uses City of Coventry Stadium. Its July/August 2012 operational days concern advertising/street-trading zones. They **do not define naming validity** or disprove Ricoh’s concurrent commercial name. No clean-name interval was fabricated.
- [Companies House charge particulars](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/01947851/charges) use Cellnet Riverside for a charge created 20 April 1998 and BT Cellnet Riverside for a document created 23 December 1999. These are dated document labels, possibly preserving older language. Delivery, discharge and satisfaction dates cannot become naming transitions.
- [West Ham’s venue history](https://www.whufc.com/en/the-club/history/stadiums/london-stadium-0) explicitly associates London Stadium with the 4 August 2016 first European fixture. This improves early usage evidence without dating adoption. A later LLP company-name change is not the venue rename.
- [Brighton’s club retrospective](https://www.brightonandhovealbion.com/media-article/a-moment-that-will-live-with-me-forever) identifies American Express Community Stadium for the 6 August 2011 match. It does not verify the secondary 22 June 2010 announcement lead or prove a continuous interval to the separately supported August 2023 change.
- [Everton’s venue announcement](https://www.hilldickinsonstadium.com/news/2025/may/16/hill-dickinson-named-as-official-stadium-naming-rights-partner-/) confirms the 16 May 2025 announcement but does not explicitly say immediate effect. [Ospreys’ shared announcement](https://www.ospreysrugby.com/news/ospreys-play-renamed-swanseacom-stadium) retains Liberty’s year-level 2005 assertion; shared text is not independent original 2005 confirmation.
